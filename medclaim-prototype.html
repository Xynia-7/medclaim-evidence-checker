Claim under review

“In DESTINY-Gastric01, T-DXd achieved an objective response rate of 51% versus 14% with physician's-choice chemotherapy; overall survival was the primary endpoint.”
Minimum evidence

The independent central-review ORR was 51% versus 14%. The stated primary endpoint was objective response—not overall survival.

Population / line

HER2-positive advanced gastric or gastro-oesophageal junction cancer in Japan and South Korea; at least two prior lines including trastuzumab.

Source boundary

Trial summary evidence. It does not expand the regulatory indication or support an individual treatment recommendation.
